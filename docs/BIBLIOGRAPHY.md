## Two notes worth a line on the slide

**Licensing.** SHRUG [10], [11] is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — non-commercial and share-alike, and derived
datasets inherit it. The underlying government data is GODL-India, which does permit commercial
use, so a commercial deployment must re-derive from MoSPI primary data rather than from SHRUG.

**Vintage.** The Economic Census figures [7], [8] are from the 2013 round. That is the most recent
complete establishment census; ASUSE [9] is the current sample survey but is not a census and does
not give village-level counts. Saying so is stronger than letting a reviewer notice the gap.
